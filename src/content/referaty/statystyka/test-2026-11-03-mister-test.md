Conformal prediction provides prediction intervals with finite-sample coverage guarantees under the sole assumption of exchangeability. In practice, however, covariates are often only partially observed, and the missingness pattern itself carries information about the response. We propose a pattern-adaptive split conformal procedure for sparse high-dimensional regression which combines imputation with reweighting of nonconformity scores within missingness patterns. We prove marginal coverage under missing-at-random mechanisms and approximate coverage conditional on the pattern, and we illustrate the method on simulated data and on a clinical data set with more than 2000 predictors.
